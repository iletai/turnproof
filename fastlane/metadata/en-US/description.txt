Turnproofs turns a room-by-room rental walkthrough into a condition report that landlord and tenant sign on the spot. Photograph each room, note the condition, flag damage, sign together, and the app seals the packet into a PDF with a SHA-256 digest you can hand over the same day.

A report, not a pile of photos
- Rooms, findings and timestamps are kept in order.
- Every finding has a photo, a condition (good, fair, damaged) and an optional note.
- A damage flag needs a note before the report can be sealed.
- The review screen shows what is missing before anyone signs.

Signed by both parties
- Tenant and landlord sign on the same phone, in the app.
- The signature page prints both signatures, names and signing time.

Sealed and verifiable
- Sealing computes a SHA-256 digest over the room records, photos and signatures. It is printed on the integrity page, in the PDF metadata and as a QR code.
- The Verify screen recomputes the digest on your device and tells you whether it matches.
- A sealed report is read-only. A correction creates a new revision.

Private by design
- No account, no analytics, no ads, no server.
- Works offline. Photos and reports stay on this device; nothing is uploaded.
- Share the PDF with AirDrop, Files, Mail or any share target you choose.

Free to try
- Your first sealed report is free, including export and sharing.
- Turnproofs Pro covers every later report: monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
- Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. Restore purchases from the paywall.
- Opening a sealed report and re-checking its digest is always free.

What it is not
Turnproofs is a record-keeping tool. It is not legal advice, and it does not decide how a deposit dispute is settled.
