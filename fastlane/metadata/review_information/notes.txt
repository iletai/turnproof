No account or sign-in is needed. Launch the app, tap + (or "Add Property"), add a unit, open it and tap "Start new inspection". Tap each room, take a photo (on a device without a camera use the Library button), set its condition, then tap "Review & Sign", "Continue to Sign", have both parties draw a signature and tap "Seal Report". The first sealed report exports for free; the second export shows the paywall (also reachable from Settings > See Plans). Test purchases use the App Store sandbox: Turnproofs Pro monthly, yearly and lifetime. All data stays on the device; the app makes no network requests besides StoreKit and works the same in every region.
