1. Main flow (no account or sign-in): launch the app; on Properties tap + (or "Add Property"), enter a label, address and your name, tap "Save unit". Open the unit, choose Move-in, Move-out or Periodic and tap "Start new inspection". Tap a room, take a photo (or pick one with the Library button on a device without a camera), set Good, Fair or Damaged, optionally add a note and "Flag as damage", then go back and repeat for the other rooms. Tap "Review & Sign" and "Continue to Sign" (damage flagged without a note blocks this step until a note is added). Both parties draw a signature and tap "Seal Report". The sealed report shows a verification code, "Verify Report" and "Share PDF". Paid content: the first sealed report exports for free; sealing a second report shows the paywall (Turnproofs Pro monthly, yearly with a 7-day free trial, or lifetime), also reachable from Settings (gear icon) > See Plans, with Restore Purchases. Use an App Store sandbox account. There is no account registration, login or user-generated content shared with others.
2. Purpose and audience: landlords, property managers and tenants who need a dated, signed record of a rental unit's condition at move-in, move-out or periodic inspections. It replaces paper checklists and loose phone photos with one report both parties sign on the spot, which reduces security-deposit disputes.
3. Setup: none. No login, credentials or sample files are needed; all features are available right after launch.
4. External services: none. All data stays on the device; the only network use is Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases. No analytics, ads, AI or third-party SDKs.
5. Regions: the app works the same in every region.
6. Regulated industry or third-party material: not applicable. The report is a record of condition; the app does not claim legal force or give legal advice.
